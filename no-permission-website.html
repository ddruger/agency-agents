About the Show

Intimate.
Unfiltered.
Too Early
to Know.

The founders nobody saw coming —
before anyone knew it.

No Permission is not a success story retrospective. It's not a highlight reel of hustle culture clichés. And it's not another founder sitting across from you telling you how they built it with a nine-figure exit already banked.

This is something different: intimate, long-form video conversations with the next generation of builders — caught at a specific, fleeting moment. After the proof exists. Before the world has caught up.

Every guest on this show was too young to have a track record and too unproven to have credibility. No one handed them a seat at the table. By any conventional measure, they weren't allowed to build what they built. They didn't ask. They built anyway. The company is real. The traction is there. And the story isn't finished yet.

"I've invested in founders like this. I've watched them get dismissed, ignored, and underestimated — and then be right anyway. No Permission exists to document that moment before the world catches up."

— Daniel Druger, Host
