About the Show

Intimate.

The Founders Nobody Saw Coming.
That Everyone Will Know.

No Permission is not a success story retrospective. It's not a highlight reel of hustle culture clichés. And it's not another founder telling you how they built it with a nine-figure exit behind them.

This is something different: intimate, authentic conversations with the next generation of builders caught at a specific, fleeting moment. After taking the leap. Now that the proof exists. Before the world has caught up.

Every guest that comes on No Permission was too young to have a track record and too unproven to have credibility. No one handed them a seat at the table. By any conventional measure, they shouldn't have been able to build what they built. They didn't ask. They built anyway. The company is real. The traction is there. And the story isn't finished yet.

"I've invested in founders like this. I've heard their stories, the challenges they faced, and how often they were written off. To then be right anyway. No Permission exists to tell their stories and document that moment before the world catches up."

— Daniel Druger, Host

Not a Show About Hustle Culture
This isn't about grinding harder or the 5am routine. It's about personal belief and that leap of faith. Founders who heard 'no', but decided to do it anyway.
02
While the Story Is Being Written
Most founder stories start from the exit, the big win. We capture it in real time, as it's happening. The traction is real, the future is open, and you're hearing it before anyone decides they're right.
03
The Next Big Thing. Before It's Big.
These aren't founders that have made it. They're the ones building the next big thing while the world is still figuring out if they're allowed to. They're early, but they're proving everyone that they're on to something.
04
Not "How I Built This"
No polished retrospectives with a decade of hindsight. These guests are still in the middle of it — the wins are real, the stakes are live, and nobody's edited out the hard parts yet.
