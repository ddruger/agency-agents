About the Show

Built Different.
Built Bold.

No Permission captures founders at a specific, fleeting moment: after the proof exists, before the world has caught up. Every guest on this show was too young to have a track record and too unproven to have credibility — and they built anyway.

These are not the famous ones. No track record. No credentials. No one handing them a seat at the table. The world hadn't decided they were allowed yet — and by any conventional measure, it wouldn't have. They didn't ask. They built.

Real revenue. Real users. Real proof. The stories are unfinished — and that's exactly what makes them worth hearing now.

Season 1 is ten USC founders, all dropping at once.
